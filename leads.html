How these work. Carta fechada — you hand a sealed written proposal to the agente de execução by the deadline; all are opened together, highest wins. Negociação particular — the AE sells by direct negotiation; you contact them and make an offer. Neither runs on e-leilões, so there are far fewer bidders. To act: I open the lot's Citius detail for the valor base, the deadline and the AE's name + email, then value it against comparables and it graduates into the main tracker.

How to find one on Citius. The executivos page has no processo search box — you must select the court shown under each processo, then set Tipo de Bem: Imóvel and Estado: Em venda, and locate the processo in the list. Two gotchas: the court ≠ the property's location (a lot at a Braga court is actually in Arco da Calheta, Madeira — flagged, excluded from Porto-metro), and the court is the comarca, not the town you might expect (e.g. Anadia is Comarca de Aveiro, not Braga). Easiest path: hit Sync and I pull each lot's valor + área + contact onto this page so you don't have to search at all.
Valores & documents. Valor base and espécie fill in on the next ⟳ Sync (I re-read each lot's Citius detail). Área, VPT and the agente-de-execução contact come from that same detail; ónus / encargos and the owed amount aren't on the public register — they're in the CRP / execution file, which is the per-lot document fetch. Citius has no per-lot URL, so each Processo ↗ opens the executivos search for you to look it up.
