Carta fechada — direto ao tribunal

The Citius declarativos notices that carry a downloadable anúncio. These are the sales where bids go straight to the court secretaria in a sealed envelope — no leiloeira, no platform, no buyer's premium. The anúncio fixes the whole deal: the property, the valor base, the 85% floor you must beat, the day and hour the envelopes are opened, and the 5% caução (cheque visado) that must travel with the proposal. How this works. Proposals are delivered to the court's secretaria before the stated hour — there is no online bidding and no extension. Each proposal needs a cheque visado à ordem da secretaria for 5% of the valor anunciado (or a bank guarantee), and postal proposals must carry CC/BI copy, NIF, marital regime and a contact or they are refused. The floor is 85% of the valor base, so the "Mínimo" column is what you actually have to beat. Finding a row back on Citius: the common key is the Referência — it prints on the Citius row as "Referência: 146287655" and is on every row here. But "Todos" returns ~16.000 registos at 10 per page, so Ctrl+F only ever searches the page you are on: select the comarca shown on the row first, then Ctrl+F the referência. Or just use 📄 anúncio (PDF), which opens the copy saved on this site the day the row was swept. That copy is the reliable one: Citius' own "Descarregar Ficheiro" address carries a q= token tied to the search session that produced it, so it stops working within the hour and cannot be bookmarked or shared. Espécie: Inventário = estate/probate · Divisão de Coisa Comum = co-owners forcing a sale · Herança = vacant estate. Charges: in these sales mortgages and penhoras do not automatically cancel the way they do in execução — read the "créditos reclamados" line and check the CRP before bidding.
