Cedofeita loja — unsold at €173,417 against €187,000, and this is the one worth remembering. Corrected four days before the close, and the result matched the corrected read: the market would not pay the reserve. Had the estimate stood, today would have looked like a bargain missed rather than a lot that was simply priced too high.
